Software Engineer at Runo · Hyderabad, India

I'm a software engineer at Runo, where I work across the stack of a sales CRM: the Flutter web admin app and its integrations, product analytics, a Chrome extension for WhatsApp Web, and the daily call-analytics pipeline.

Before Runo I was at NMOLD, a manufacturer, rebuilding its inventory system in Flutter, Express and MongoDB and connecting its factory machines to it. While finishing my B.Tech in Information Technology at Panipat Institute of Engineering & Technology, I built the college's exam and admin portals part-time and took freelance client work: an OMR sheet reader that processed more than 90,000 sheets and a blockchain donation tracker for an NGO.

I also publish two small Flutter packages on pub.dev, image_hotspot andfloor_plan_builder.

The selected work on the home page links to longer case studies, and the résumé has the full history.
