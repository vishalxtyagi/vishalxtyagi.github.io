I'm a software engineer at Runo, where I work across the stack of a sales CRM: the Flutter web admin app and its integrations, product analytics, a Chrome extension for WhatsApp Web, and the daily call-analytics pipeline.

I studied for a B.Tech in Information Technology at Panipat Institute of Engineering & Technology from 2021 to 2025. From my second year I was the college's paid, part-time software developer: the only developer on its exam, feedback, ticketing and bus-tracking systems, which I kept running until I graduated. Alongside that I took freelance client work, including an OMR sheet reader that processed more than 90,000 sheets and a blockchain donation tracker for an NGO.

In my last three semesters I also worked part-time at NMOLD, a manufacturer, rebuilding its inventory system in Flutter, Express and MongoDB and connecting its factory machines to it. I joined Runo full-time after graduating in June 2025.

I also publish two small Flutter packages on pub.dev, image_hotspot and floor_plan_builder.

The selected work on the home page links to longer case studies, and the résumé has the full history.
